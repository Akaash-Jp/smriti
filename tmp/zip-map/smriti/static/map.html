Brihadisvara Temple

1010 CE • Chola Dynasty (Raja Raja Chola I)

Known locally as Thanjai Periya Kovil, this granite temple rises 66 metres high and was built over a thousand years ago with interlocking stones and no mortar.

What materials & tools are used?

Interlocking carved granite blocks, Chola frescoes using natural mineral pigments

Who keeps this tradition alive today?

Bharatanatyam temple dance, Swamimalai lost-wax bronze casting, and Carnatic percussion

What story does it carry?

How Chola builders raised an 80-tonne granite capstone to the top of the vimana tower using an earthen ramp.
